## 説明文（4,000 文字まで）

```
"Where should we eat?"
"Who's up first?"
"What's the running order?"

When you can't decide, give it a spin.

◆ Decide one thing with the roulette
Add your options, press Spin, and the wheel turns, slows down, and lands on the result. Use between 2 and 24 items, and flick the wheel to spin it, too.

◆ Settle everyone's order
Press Shuffle in the Random order tab and the order appears one place at a time. Perfect for running order, seating, and chore duty. Press again to draw a new order anytime.

◆ Good for
Picking who organizes the party, choosing where to have lunch, setting the running order, assigning chores, forfeits, or standing in for a coin toss. Free, no sign-up.

◆ Make the items yours
Give each item a color or an emoji. Save the lists you use often with a name and load them whenever you need them.

◆ Share the result
"Share result" sends an image of the result together with the text. The X and LINE buttons open a post with the result already filled in. Send it to a group chat and everyone sees the same result.

◆ Sound and feel
Spin sound, haptics, how the stopped slice glows, and a 3D wheel. Fine-tune it all in Settings.

◆ App icons to choose from
Pick from Neon, Pastel, Spinner, and Rings (iPhone and iPad).

◆ For iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro
It follows Dark Mode and larger text settings, too.

◆ Your data stays on your device
No account, no network, no ads, no in-app purchases. Your items are still there the next time you open the app. (They're included in device backups to iCloud or a computer and are erased when you delete the app.)

For the days you just can't decide.
```
